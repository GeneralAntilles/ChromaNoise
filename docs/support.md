# Support

Found a bug or have an idea? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/GeneralAntilles/ChromaNoise/issues).
Please include your device model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**How do I make my own sound?**
Drag across the bars to paint a spectrum. Left is low frequencies,
right is high. Tap **Save** to keep it under My Sounds.

**Can I rename or delete a saved sound?**
Touch and hold it.

**Will it keep playing with the screen locked?**
Yes. Use the lock screen or Control Center to pause, or set the sleep
timer (the moon button) to fade out and stop.

**What does Swell do?**
It slowly raises and lowers the volume, like waves. Depth sets how far
it dips; Period sets how long one rise and fall takes.
