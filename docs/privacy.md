# Privacy Policy

*Effective November 6, 2026*

ChromaNoise doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app contains
  no third-party SDKs.
- **No network access.** The app generates all sound on your device
  and never sends anything over the internet.
- **Your sounds stay on your device.** The sounds you save, and the
  last sound you played, are stored only in the app's own storage on
  your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you've chosen to share analytics with Apple, Apple may provide the
developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). These
can't be used to identify you.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this
page with a new effective date.

Questions? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/GeneralAntilles/ChromaNoise/issues).
